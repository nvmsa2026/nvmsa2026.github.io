**Session Chair:** Yu Liang  

This panel brings together women researchers at different career stages to share their academic journeys, research experiences, and lessons learned. Through experience sharing and open discussion, the session aims to provide practical advice for students and early-career researchers while fostering mentorship, networking, and an inclusive research community. Everyone is welcome to participate.
