Registration Policy
  • At least one unique author must register for NVMSA'26 at the full registration rate (i.e., non-student) for the full/short conference paper to appear in the proceedings. For student demo/poster presenter without a conference paper, a student registration can cover a demo/poster abstract. If you have already registered for NVMSA 2026 and find that you are unable to attend the conference, please notify NVMSA 2026 Registration Chair in writing to rtcsa.nvmsa@gmail.com to cancel your registration and request a refund if applicable. 
  • Cancellation deadline:  July 12th, 2026 AoE in terms of the receipt time of the request email.
  • Cancellation fee:
    • 50% of the registration fee before the cancellation deadline (July 12th 2026 AoE);
    • 100% of the registration fee after the cancellation deadline (i.e., no refund).
  • All refunds will be made after the symposium.
Note: All dates are in Anywhere on Earth (AoE) time zone. Please ensure your registration is completed before the deadline to enjoy early bird rates.
