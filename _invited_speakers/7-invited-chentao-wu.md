**Abstract**: With the surging demand for computing power in cloud computing and artificial intelligence, Compute Express Link (CXL) technology has emerged as a core solution for building large-scale memory pools, thanks to its ultra-low latency and cache coherency capabilities. However, while CXL memory pooling breaks down physical node boundaries and significantly improves memory resource utilization, it also introduces severe reliability challenges. The pooled architecture drastically expands the "blast radius" of memory failures; the failure of a single CXL memory module or controller can trigger simultaneous crashes across multiple attached nodes. Furthermore, traditional cross-node fault-tolerance mechanisms incur excessive network overhead, severely undermining the low-latency performance advantages of CXL. This presentation focuses on CXL memory reliability, outlining our team's research into areas such as ECC coding and memory mirroring for CXL memory.

**Bio**: Chentao Wu is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. He serves as the Deputy Director of the Institute of Intelligent Software and Systems and the Deputy Director of the State Key Laboratory of Digital Finance (under preparation). He is a recipient of national-level young talent honors and serves as a Chief Scientist for the National Key R&D Program. He holds dual Ph.D. degrees from Huazhong University of Science and Technology and Virginia Commonwealth University. He is a Standing Committee member and Deputy Secretary-General of the Technical Committee on Computer Architecture of the China Computer Federation (CCF), as well as the Chair of the Technical Committee on Storage Systems of the Shanghai Computer Society. His research focuses primarily on cloud storage and big data storage systems. He has led over 20 projects, including those funded by the National Key R&D Program, the National Natural Science Foundation of China (Key Program), and the Shanghai Natural Science Foundation. He has published more than 150 papers in prestigious journals and conferences such as IEEE TC, IEEE TPDS, IEEE TDSC, IEEE TMC, OSDI, FAST, MICRO, ATC, HPCA, and DAC. His research achievements have been recognized with one national award, two provincial/ministerial-level awards, four academic society awards, and multiple best paper awards.
