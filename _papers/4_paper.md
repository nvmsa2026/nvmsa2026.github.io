title: "Wear-Aware Dynamic Zone Mapping for Lifetime Enhancement in Zoned Namespace SSDs"

**Abstract**: Zoned Namespace (ZNS) SSDs have emerged as a high-efficiency storage paradigm by offloading media management to the host; however, this architectural shift introduces significant challenges for flash endurance. The standard Zone Reset operation, which serves as the primary mechanism for reclaiming storage space, inherently erases all physical blocks mapped to a zone, regardless of their internal utilization. This leads to ``premature erasures,'' where unprogrammed blocks are unnecessarily cycled, exacerbating wear across the NAND media. Furthermore, while the previous method proposes a novel zone reset to eliminate unnecessary erase operations, the issue of unbalanced erase distribution arises. Such an observation motivates this paper to propose a dynamic block-mapping strategy that leverages a just-in-time allocation policy to break the rigid binding between logical zones and physical blocks. By exposing fine-grained device metrics, including a block-erase-count record and a zone-level reset-hint table, to the host, the proposed strategy effectively bridges the semantic gap between the host-side file system and the underlying NAND media. In addition, an adaptive, wear-aware reclamation policy and a Free block refreshment are integrated to dynamically prioritize low-wear zones during garbage collection and proactively migrate cold data, collectively ensuring uniform endurance distribution and maximizing the overall physical lifespan of the SSD. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed dynamic mapping strategy significantly improves overall wear leveling and reduces the maximum block erase count by 29.33% with minimal system overhead compared to state-of-the-art static mapping techniques.
