**Abstract**: Hybrid SSDs provide flexible trade-offs between performance and capacity, but their address translation still suffers from large metadata overhead and extra flash accesses caused by limited SSD DRAM. This paper presents a lifecycle-aware address mapping framework that adapts translation representations to data stability across different lifecycle stages. The framework exploits migration as an opportunity to reorganize physical layout and rebuild compact translation structures, while a mode-aware cache dynamically regulates SSD DRAM space among competing cache components. Experimental results show that the proposed method effectively reduces response time.
